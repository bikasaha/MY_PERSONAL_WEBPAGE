Actively Looking for Postdoctoral Position

Bikash Saha a Ph.D. candidate at the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur, supervised by Prof. Sandeep Kumar Shukla and Prof. Urbi Chatterjee. He is a recipient of the prestigious C3iHub Ph.D. Fellowship for cybersecurity research. His research lies at the intersection of cybersecurity and artificial intelligence, with a focus on offensive security, malware behavior understanding, threat intelligence extraction, and security compliance analysis.

Before joining IIT Kanpur, he completed his M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Bhilai, where I worked under Dr. Rishi Ranjan Singh. I also worked as a Software Developer at Sterlite Technologies Limited.
